#### simplesRates.ts against topic 1

Checked every band of Anexos III, IV and V: nominal rate, deduction, band limit, and the Cofins, PIS/Pasep and ISS shares. Checked BAND5_CAP (thresholds 14,92537 and 12,5; Cofins 19,28% and 30,13%; PIS 4,18% and 6,54%) and ISS_CAP = 5.

Result: no difference in any value. The table values match LC 123 exactly.

New values (not in simplesRates.ts today; see the topic 1 rows):

- IRPJ, CSLL, CPP shares of every band of Anexos III and V, and IRPJ and CSLL of Anexo IV (Anexo IV has no CPP).
- BAND5_CAP: Anexo III IRPJ 6,02%, CSLL 5,26%, CPP 65,26%; Anexo IV IRPJ 31,33%, CSLL 32,00%.
- Anexo V has no band 5 cap (the ISS share never passes 5%).

Differences in behaviour, not in table values:

1. Band 6 and the ISS. The code has no ISS share in band 6 ("above R$ 3.6 million they leave the DAS (art. 13-A)"), so `simplesIssRate` returns null and `simplesRate` returns only the band 6 effective rate. The official rule is different: the sublimit is tested on the revenue of the current calendar year (RBA), not on the RBT12. With RBT12 above 3.600.000,00 and the year's revenue under the sublimit, the DAS still has the ISS, at the band 5 formula applied to the RBT12, capped at 5% with the excess spread to the federal taxes (CGSN140, art. 21 III b; MANUAL Exemplo 8: 18,60% + 6,09968%). Above the sublimit the ISS stays in the DAS until the impediment takes effect (next month if the excess is above 20%, else next year), at the band 5 rate at 3.600.000,00 (CGSN140, art. 24 I b). So for a band 6 company that still pays ISS in the DAS, the code understates the total rate and returns no retained ISS rate.
2. Retained ISS window. CGSN140 art. 27 I takes the band from "a receita bruta acumulada nos 12 (doze) meses que antecederem o mês anterior ao da prestação". `simplesIssRate` takes an RBT12 from the caller, so the caller must pass that window. It is not a table difference; check the caller in Task 2.
3. RBT12 = 0. The code uses the band 1 nominal rate. This matches CGSN140 art. 21, parágrafo único (RBT12 = R$ 1,00). No difference.
4. Fator R precision (for the new `fatorR` of Task 2): the PGDAS-D truncates r to two decimals (0,2774 becomes 0,27, MANUAL 8.2.1).

#### annexRule.ts against topic 3

The file says it is NotaFlow's own table and that no official CNAE table exists. That is right: an official CNAE list per annex is not confirmed. The CNAE codes named below come from the CNAE structure, not from LC 123; check each one before a change.

1. Comment error: line 15 puts architecture (7111100) under "§5º-I VI". Since LC 155/2016, architecture is §5º-B XVIII (item VI no longer names it). The result is the same (Fator R, through §5º-M I).
2. Missing Fator R activity: dental prosthesis (§5º-B XX "odontologia e prótese dentária"; §5º-D XIV "serviços de prótese em geral"). The line 5 comment names "prostheses", but no prefix covers CNAE 3250-7/06 (serviços de prótese dentária), so it falls to Anexo III.
3. Missing Fator R activity: agronomy (§5º-I VI "agronomia"), CNAE 7490-1/03. It falls to Anexo III.
4. Missing Fator R activity: journalism (§5º-I X "jornalismo e publicidade"), for example CNAE 6391-7/00 and 9002-7/01. Only publicity (731) is listed.
5. Missing Fator R activities with no prefix: §5º-I VIII "perícia, leilão e avaliação" and §5º-D IX "empresas montadoras de estandes para feiras". Their CNAE codes: not confirmed.
6. Default for intellectual services. §5º-I XII sends every other intellectual, technical, scientific, sporting, artistic or cultural service to Anexo V (Fator R) when no other annex names it. The code defaults every unlisted CNAE to Anexo III. CGSN140 art. 25 §1º III m keeps Anexo III only for services that are not intellectual.
7. §5º-E activities (communication; intermunicipal and interstate transport of cargo and passengers) are Anexo III without the ISS share and with the ICMS share of Anexo I. The code returns plain 'III' for them.
8. Anexo IV prefixes that the law does not name in clear words, not confirmed: '812' includes 8122-2/00 (imunização e controle de pragas), which is not plainly "limpeza ou conservação"; '8012900' (transporte de valores) is not plainly "vigilância"; and CNAE 8020-0 (security monitoring) is not in the list. '43' includes installation services (for example 4321-5/00, 4322-3) that may be "instalação, de reparos e de manutenção em geral" of §5º-B IX (Anexo III) and not "obras de engenharia" of §5º-C I.

All other prefixes agree with the wording of LC 123 art. 18 and CGSN140 art. 25 §1º.

### From part B (corrections and surprises)

Surprises, against common knowledge:

1. LC 238/2026 was published today (DOU 21.10.2026, edição extra). The MEI limit becomes R$ 110.000,00 for 2027 and R$ 140.000,00 from 2028, with up to 2 employees from 2027. On 2026-10-21 the limit in effect is still R$ 81.000,00 with 1 employee. The raise depends on the budget laws of 2027 to 2029 (art. 3). The guide must show the date of each value.
2. LC 224/2025 raised the Lucro Presumido presumption by 10% (32% becomes 35,2% for services), but only on revenue above R$ 5 mi a year (R$ 1,25 mi a quarter). It applies to IRPJ from 2026-01-01 and to CSLL from 2026-04-01. Small service companies do not change.
3. The Simples rule for the 11% INSS retention is in IN RFB 2.110/2022 arts. 166 and 167, not art. 117.
4. The national NFS-e has no single national cancellation deadline. Each municipality sets it, and it can also block a cancel by value, by missing tomador or after the tax is paid. Only the substitution has a stated national maximum (2 years).
5. No software developer or consultant occupation is in Res. CGSN 140 Anexo XI. Those users cannot be MEI.

Not confirmed (7 items): ITG 1000 position (CFC outside the allowed domains); DCTFWeb "sem movimento" duty for a Simples company with no payroll and no pró-labore; full-books duty of a sociedade simples; an official worked example of the 35,2% rate; a national 730-day cap for cancellation; PIS cumulative rule in Lei 10.637 art. 8 (not read); end date of PIS/COFINS under LC 214/2025 (not read).

### From part C (notes for the guide)

1. Do not write "an e-CNPJ A1 lasts 1 year" as a permanent fact. The ICP-Brasil plan ends the A1 type on 2029-03-03 (CG ICP-Brasil FAQ).
2. Do not write "the free emissor needs a certificate". It does not. User and password work for any company; gov.br works for MEI.
3. Do not write "a Simples company must fill IBS/CBS in 2026". The law and Ato nº 4/2026 say 2027-01-01.
4. Do not write "a Simples company pays IBS/CBS outside the DAS in 2026". The option starts for 2027, by semester, chosen in September or March (LC 123 art. 13 §10).
5. Two sources are outside the agreed list: cgibs.gov.br (the official Comitê Gestor do IBS site) and gov.br/sped. They are official.
